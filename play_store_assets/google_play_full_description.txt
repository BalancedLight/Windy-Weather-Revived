Windy Weather Revived brings a classic windmill live wallpaper to modern Android devices with live Open-Meteo weather, dynamic skies, selectable texture quality, moon phases calculated on the device, and optional experimental local Samsung device weather support on compatible Samsung devices.

Windy Weather Revived is an independent, unofficial open-source project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Samsung Electronics. Samsung and related product names are trademarks of their respective owners.

Approximate-location weather is optional and off until you accept the in-app disclosure. Rounded coordinates are sent securely to Open-Meteo only for app functionality. The app is permanently free and ad-free.
