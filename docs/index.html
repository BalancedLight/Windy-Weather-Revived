Windy Weather Revived

Windy Weather Revived is a revival and rebuild of the old Samsung S3 secret live wallpaper service for modern smartphones. It includes Open-Meteo compatibility, experimental Samsung weather integration on eligible devices, and a full Kotlin port while retaining the original experience.
